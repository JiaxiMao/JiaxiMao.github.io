Projects

Predicting subsurface temperature across Great Britain

Using the BGS Digital Geothermal Catalogue, I modelled 5,367 equilibrium temperature measurements from 108 boreholes. Whole 50 km regions were held out to test how the model performs in places it has never seen.

Gradient boosting reached a mean error of 2.64 °C (R² 0.945), 29% lower than a linear geothermal-gradient baseline.

Python, scikit-learn, pandas, spatial cross-validation

Mapping and predicting PM₂.₅ across London

I combined GLA air-quality, traffic and green-cover data on a 500 m grid (6,670 cells), then compared linear regression, random forest and XGBoost on held-out 5 km blocks.

XGBoost explained 60% of the spatial variation in held-out areas. Road exposure was the strongest predictor and green cover was negatively associated with PM₂.₅.

Python, GeoPandas, XGBoost, scikit-learn, GIS

Code and write-up on GitHub
